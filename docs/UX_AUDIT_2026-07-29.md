# UX Audit — 2026-07-29 (findings only, no code changes)

Scope requested: **audit first, then fix**. Read-only pass over the live app (logged in as CY6001, market closed). This pass covered **Dashboard, Analytics, Alerts, My Patterns**. Browser extension dropped before **My Record, Settings, Journal, Reports, Chat, My Rules** — those are still pending a second pass.

**Headline:** the biggest problem is NOT styling — it's that **the same facts tell different stories on different pages**. Loss-limit, alert counts, and product verdicts contradict each other. For a "mirror" product whose entire value is *"trust the facts we show you,"* self-contradiction is the #1 credibility leak. Fix the data/copy truth first, polish second.

---

## P0 — Cross-page contradictions (trust-breaking)

### 1. Loss limit shows two different limits and two different verdicts
- Dashboard top bar: **"LOSS LIMIT 45% · ₹11,342.49 / ₹25,000.00"**, risk pill **"On Track"** (green).
- Dashboard alert (same screen): **"Constitution Violation — daily loss limit breached: ₹11,342 lost of ₹3,000 limit (378%)."**
- So the same day is simultaneously "45% of limit, On Track" and "378% of limit, breached." Two different loss limits (₹25,000 vs ₹3,000) are live at once.
- **Why it matters:** the risk pill says safe while the alert says catastrophic. User can't tell which limit is real. Pick ONE source of truth for the daily loss limit and make the pill + the alert agree.

### 2. Alert count + verdict disagree across three surfaces
- Dashboard "BEHAVIORAL ALERTS": **5 HIGH** alerts listed.
- Alerts page header: **"14 total · 11 danger."**
- Alerts page body (Unreviewed tab): **"Clean session — No active behavioral alerts. You're trading with discipline — keep it up."**
- Dashboard + My Patterns behavior verdict: **"Normal."**
- **Why it matters:** on the SAME page, the header says 11 danger while the body praises a "clean session / trading with discipline." That's not just inconsistent — praising discipline on an 11-danger, −₹11,342 day is the opposite of the intended mirror. Reconcile the counts and never show the "clean/keep it up" empty-state while danger alerts exist.

### 3. Analytics: NRML is both "biggest leak" and "strongest product type"
- Overview hero: **"BIGGEST LEAK: NRML"** and **"WHERE TO FOCUS: your biggest drain is nrml (−₹11,342)."**
- Product Mix card (same page): **"NRML is your strongest product type this period (−₹11,342.00)."**
- **Why it matters:** one product, labeled best and worst on one screen; and calling a −₹11,342 loss "strongest" is just wrong copy. The "strongest product" line is auto-generated and breaks when there's a single losing product. Fix the wording logic (strongest ≠ most-negative) and de-duplicate the leak/focus/product story.

## P1 — Verdict logic vs. severity (misleading reassurance)

### 4. "Behavior Risk: Normal" while critical/danger alerts fired today
- Dashboard pill and My Patterns score both say **"Normal."** My Patterns score **"28"** with drivers **"session meltdown (danger)"** and **"constitution violation (critical)."**
- A day with a *critical* constitution violation + *danger* meltdown reading as "Normal" makes the headline verdict feel disconnected from the alert stream. Either the score should move with severity, or the label needs to stop saying "Normal" on a critical day.

### 5. Duplicate insight on Analytics Overview
- "BIGGEST LEAK" (NRML, −11,342) and "WHERE TO FOCUS" (nrml, −11,342) say the same thing in two cards. Collapse or differentiate.

### 6. Dashboard has two competing alert surfaces
- Left: "BEHAVIORAL ALERTS" list. Right: floating cards (WEDNESDAY DANGER DAY, REVENGE WINDOW, "Wednesday is YOUR worst day"). No visible explanation of how they differ; feels redundant and splits attention. The right column then sits mostly empty below 3 cards (layout imbalance).

## P2 — Clarity / semantics

### 7. My Patterns score bars — unclear scale + color meaning
- Score "28" of unknown max; Tilt 7 / Risk 12 / Discipline 27 / Strategy health 0. Bars are **green even for "Risk 12,"** so it's unclear whether green = good or green = the metric's fill. Risk shown in the same green as Discipline reads as "risk is fine." Add a scale/legend and make risk-colored semantics consistent (bad = red/amber).

### 8. Alert-severity monotony → alert fatigue
- Every Dashboard alert badge is **"HIGH"** in the same red. No visual gradation between info/caution/danger/critical. When everything is HIGH, nothing is. Use the real 4-level severity vocabulary visually.

### 9. Equity curve looks broken with sparse data
- The top Overview chart is near-empty (one day: axis "₹11.3k", "29 Jul") with no clear "not enough history yet" state — reads as a rendering failure rather than a cold-start.

## Second pass (2026-07-29, later) — remaining 6 pages reviewed

All 10 pages now covered. Findings:

### N1 — "Today" disagrees across pages (P1, confirm live)
- **Dashboard**: 0 trades · ₹0 today. **Chat** top strip: "Today · +₹0 · 0 trades · 14 alerts · safe". **My Rules** "Today vs your rules": **17 trades · −₹11,342 · 378% of loss limit**, with rule violations timestamped 09:32am–02:44pm *today*.
- Same "today", two different truths. Either the day-boundary/session-window definition differs between the risk/rules endpoints and the dashboard trade-stats endpoint, OR Dashboard+Chat lost the active session's data. Also: "14 alerts today" while "0 trades today" is internally inconsistent (alerts with no trades).
- **Can't fully disambiguate with the market closed** — resolve during tomorrow's live validation (when there ARE trades today). Same family as P0 #1: truth surfaced inconsistently.

### N2 — Journal misleading-empty (P2)
- Header "**19 entries total**" but the default **30d** view renders "**No entries match these filters — try removing a filter**". The page looks broken/empty on load despite having 19 entries.
- Fix: default to a window that shows entries (or "All"), or make the empty-state say "19 entries, none in the last 30 days — try 90d/All".

### N3 — Report/brief features dormant without Celery beat (note, not a bug)
- **Reports** ("No reports yet — saved when daily briefs are sent") never populate until the Celery **worker + beat** run. Same dependency as real-time alerts. Expected; flagged so it isn't mistaken for a UI bug.

### Pages that are GOOD (no findings)
- **My Record** — best page in the app: factual, on-philosophy (raw P&L, strongest/weakest window, "buckets under 5 trades marked too thin"). Reference standard for the rest.
- **My Rules** — strong: "Today vs your rules" with real limits (₹3,000 / 5 trades, breach %), rule-violation log, tighten/loosen. This is the reference TRUTH the Dashboard hero must match (validates P0 #1).
- **Settings** — clean, well-structured (broker + Profile/Notifications/Insights/Danger Zone).
- **Chat / AI Coach** — clean, suggested prompts, SEBI disclaimer present.

### Cross-check for the P0 loss-limit fix
- My Rules already shows the correct ₹3,000 / 5. Confirms Fix #1's target: the Dashboard hero must show the same, not the hardcoded 25,000 / 10.
