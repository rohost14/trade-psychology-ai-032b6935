Layout & responsive

Four breakpoints. The canvas caps at 1440 and centres — above that the ground simply shows as a wider margin. No ultra-wide tier.

Breakpoints

Deliberately four, not six.

NameRange NavigationCanvasContent
mobile< 640 Bottom bar, 56px, 5 targets Full bleed, no radius, no side margin Single column. Tables become two-line rows
tablet640 – 1023 Icon rail, 64px 16px gutter, radius 12 Metrics wrap to 2×2. Tables drop LTP and Chg%
laptop1024 – 1439 Full sidebar, 244px 24px gutter, fills width Full tables, metrics in one row
desktop≥ 1440 Full sidebar, 244px Caps at 1440, centred Identical to laptop — nothing reflows

Nothing changes above 1440 except the size of the margin. A trader on a 27″ monitor gets the same layout as one on a 14″ laptop, just with more space around it. The alternative — letting an eight-column table stretch to 2560px — puts the symbol and its P&L two feet apart, and reading across that gap is exactly the error a P&L table exists to prevent.

Nothing is hidden behind a tap. Every figure on the laptop layout is on the mobile one — the arrangement changes, the content does not. A shorter mobile version is a different product, not a smaller one.

The table is the only thing that genuinely restructures. Six columns cannot survive 390px, so symbol and P&L take the top line and quantity, entry and LTP fold into a caption underneath. The two figures being compared stay in a column, which was the property that mattered about the table in the first place. At tablet the table keeps its columns but drops LTP and Chg%, both derivable from what is left.

Mobile navigation is a flat bottom bar of five equal targets — no floating action button. A raised centre button implies one dominant action per screen; these screens are for reading.

Rules that hold at every size

Hit targets 32px minimum, 44px on mobile. The alert response buttons — “I stood down” / “Took it anyway” / “Not useful” — are the one place this product asks for input at all, so they get the 44px treatment on every breakpoint, not just below 640.

Horizontal scrolling is never the page. If something must scroll sideways it does so inside its own container with a visible edge, and it is never the primary content of a screen.

The canvas is one scroll context. No inner panes with their own scrollbars except the sidebar, which is fixed and scrolls independently because its length is content-dependent.

Sticky is limited to the page header row. Table headers do not stick — a sticky header on a five-row table is motion for its own sake.
