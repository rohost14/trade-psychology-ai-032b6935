Forms & controls

This product asks for very little input by design — a trader who has to type is a trader who stops using it. So there are few controls, and the ones that exist are mostly about limits the reader sets once and then lives with.

Text input

The session stops when this is hit.

Must be at least ₹1,000. A limit of zero would stop you before your first trade.

Set when you connected. Disconnect to change it.

Errors say what to do. “Invalid input” is a scolding; “must be at least ₹1,000, a limit of zero would stop you before your first trade” is an instruction. Money inputs are monospaced so a mistyped digit is visible.

Toggle

Accent when on — never green. A toggle being on is a state, not a gain, and green is spent entirely on money.

Settings are rows on the page, not cards. They are a list the reader scans for the one thing they came to change; boxing each one triples the vertical space and makes scanning slower.

Destructive

Delete your account

This removes your profile, rules, trades and journal permanently. Your records at Zerodha are unaffected. Export your data first if you want a copy.

Irreversible actions are gated by typing, not by a second click. A confirm dialog is muscle memory after the third time; typing your own broker ID cannot be done by accident. The state of the input is the gate — the button stays disabled until it matches.
