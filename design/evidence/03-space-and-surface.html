Small on purpose. Large radii read friendly, and this system is not trying to be friendly — it is trying to be trusted. 8px is the largest a container gets; the full pill is reserved for badges, where the shape is doing semantic work rather than decorative work.

Only three levels, and the default is flat with a hairline. A shadow here means “this floats above the page”, so if everything has one, nothing does.

The container rule

A container has to earn itself. Give one to a thing that is separable — a single metric, one verdict, one alert. Withhold it from a set the reader scans and compares.
