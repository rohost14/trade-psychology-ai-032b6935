States

Loading, empty, error, offline and success. These are where a product actually feels unfinished, and they are always the screens a design leaves out. Specified here as first-class components so they cannot be improvised.

Loading — skeleton, not a spinner

Content loads as a skeleton; an action loads as a spinner. A skeleton says “something specific is arriving and it will be shaped like this”, which keeps the layout from jumping when it lands. Skeletons take the shape of the real content — never a grey box standing in for a whole page.

Empty — the cause, not the absence

▤

No completed trades yet

Kite only shares today’s trades, so your history starts empty. Import your Console tradebook and Analytics fills in immediately.

An empty state has three obligations: say what is missing, say why, and offer the one action that fixes it.

“No data” meets none of them. It reads as a dead end and leaves the reader to guess whether the app is broken, whether they did something wrong, or whether this is simply how it starts.

Error — what happened, whose problem, what next

!

We couldn’t load your rules

They are still saved and the engine is still enforcing them — this is only a display problem. Try again rather than re-entering them.

Never a status code, never a stack, never “something went wrong”. The reader needs to know whether their data is affected or only their view — and here it is only the view, so the copy says so before offering the retry.

A failed request is never drawn as an empty state. Rendering “no trades yet” after a request failed tells a trader with years of history that they have none. It is the single most damaging mistake in this whole set.

Offline & degraded

⚠ You’re offline — showing last known data. We’ll reconnect automatically.
● Live prices paused — reconnect your broker to resume.
Offline is a persistent banner, not a toast: the condition lasts, so the message must too. A toast is for something that has just happened and is now finished.

Success

✓ Rules tightened. Effective immediately.

Success is a toast that names what changed and when it applies. “Saved” alone makes the reader go and check.

✓

Imported 1,284 trades

Spanning 14 March to 4 August. Analytics, Edge and Habits are populated. No alerts were raised for back-dated trades.

The larger form is for a success worth reviewing rather than acknowledging — an import that changed a lot deserves a receipt, not a toast that vanishes in four seconds.
