Typography

Three faces, each with a job it does not share. The serif is the reason this reads as a written record rather than a dashboard — a verdict set at 40px in Source Serif says considered, which is the entire positioning of the product.

Aa

Source Serif 4 — display

Page titles and verdicts only. Never body, never UI chrome. Its scarcity is what gives it weight.

Aa

Inter — interface

Everything else. Chosen for density and legibility at 12–14px, where most of a trading interface actually lives.

123

DM Mono — figures

Every number, without exception. Tabular by default, so columns of money align with no extra work.

Two of these three are already in the app. Inter and DM Mono ship today; only the serif is new. That is deliberate — a design system whose adoption begins with three font swaps is a design system that does not get adopted.
