Badges & severity

Severity has three levels and the system has one colour for risk. Those two facts are reconciled by strength, not hue — solid, tint, neutral. Reaching for a fourth colour to fill the middle is what makes an interface feel busy.

Severity — the three strengths

HIGH SEVERITY

Solid

Costing real money now, and the reader should act. Reserved — if everything is high, the fill stops meaning anything.

MED SEVERITY

Tint

Same hue, quieter. Present and worth reading, not worth interrupting for.

LOW SEVERITY

Neutral

Recorded so the pattern is visible over time. Deliberately recedes — a low-severity badge in colour is a lie about its importance.

Why not amber for medium? Because an amber badge and a red badge sitting in one list read as two different topics, not two intensities of one. Three strengths of a single hue keep them on the same axis, which is what severity actually is.
