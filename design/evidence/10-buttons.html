Buttons

Four ranks. The rank is chosen by consequence, not by how much attention the button wants — which is why there is exactly one primary per view.

Ranks

primary
The one action this view exists for. Never two on a screen.
secondary
A real alternative the reader might reasonably take.
quiet
Dismissal and retreat. Carries no border so it never competes.
danger
Irreversible only. Not “important” — irreversible.

Sizes

sm · 30
md · 38
lg · 46

States

default
hover
focus
loading
disabled
Loading keeps the label. “Saving…” rather than a bare spinner, because a spinner alone drops the one piece of information the reader wants: what is happening and whether it is the thing they asked for.

Touch targets

44 × 44 min

An icon button’s hit area is 44px even when the icon is 16px. Pad it and pull the padding back with a negative margin so the layout does not move — the target grows, the design does not.
