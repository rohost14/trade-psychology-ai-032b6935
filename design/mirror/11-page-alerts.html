The row is the whole alert. There is no card, and there is no detail page you must open. Claim, evidence, your own history with it, and the response all sit on one full-width row — because the moment a trader has to click through to find out whether this has cost them money before, they stop reading alerts.

Three buttons, and the third one is the important one. Fifty-five alerts have fired and twelve were answered. “Not useful” is what keeps the other two honest: without a way to say the alert was wrong, a trader who disagrees just ignores the whole surface. An answered row dims in place and shows what happened next — that is the only argument for answering the next one.
