Brand teal is not a money colour and profit green is not a UI colour. They sit close enough in hue that the separation has to be enforced by rule rather than by eye: the moment teal also means “up”, a trader can no longer find their P&L by colour alone on a page that has nine teal links on it.

Severity

critical
Daily loss limit breached CRITICAL

Solid badge, coral rail. Reserved for a rule that is already broken.

danger
Size Escalation DANGER

Transparent badge, coral rail. Money is at risk right now.

caution
Early Exit CAUTION

No rail. A pattern worth knowing, not worth interrupting for.

info
Cooldown honoured INFO

Teal. Something went right, or something changed. Never money.

The rail is a rule, not an accent. The shipping Dashboard draws a coral rail on the first alert in the list only, which a trader reads as “most recent” rather than “most serious”. Here it means danger-or-worse on every surface, or it is not drawn.
