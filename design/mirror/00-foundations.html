Foundations

Light, cool, and layered. Two things drive every choice below: the neutral ramp carries no warmth at all, and content sits on surfaces that are visibly above the page rather than the same colour as it.

Depth — the three levels

Level 0

Ground

The page. Never holds content directly — it exists to be the thing cards sit on.

#EEF1F5

Level 1

Surface

Cards. Border and shadow, always both — never one alone.

#FFFFFF · --sh1

Level 2

Inset

Wells, table headers, chart tracks, input fields. Pressed into the surface.

#E4E9F0

Ground to surface is a 12-point luminance step, plus a border, plus a shadow. The shipping app steps #F6F5F3 to #FFFFFF — four points, no shadow — which is exactly why its cards disappear into the page. Three signals rather than one, because any single one is invisible on a dim laptop panel viewed at an angle.

Shadows are blue-black, rgba(15,23,36,…), never neutral black: a grey shadow on a cool page turns the card edge muddy.

A card wraps a subject. Hairlines divide repetition inside it. On the right, three containers of equal weight say the rows are three separate ideas, the eye has to cross a boundary for every position, and there is nothing left to mark where the section itself ends. On the left one card says “these belong together” and 1px #E8EDF3 says “and here is the next one”.

If a card ever needs to go inside a card, it should be a hairline group or an inset well instead.

No warm grey anywhere. The old ramp was built on warm paper, which under warm ink reads as stationery — the single biggest reason the previous light theme looked unfinished. Every neutral here carries a blue cast, so white cards read as brighter than the page rather than merely different from it.

Brand teal and profit green are close in hue and must never swap jobs. Green is money. Teal is everything that is not money. The moment teal also means “up”, P&L stops being findable by colour on a page carrying nine teal links.
