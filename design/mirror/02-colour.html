Colour direction — three options

The current set is about 2% chromatic: four badges, six figures, one nav item. Everything else is neutral, and the brand never appears above 13px — so teal reads as a link colour rather than an identity. Removing the cards is what removed the tinted surfaces; that was the accidental cost of the fix.

The smaller moves are where the remaining flatness actually lives. A 22px tinted icon on every section header puts the brand and the severity system at a readable size instead of only at 10px. The table header moves from grey inset to #E4F3F1, which is the single largest neutral area on most screens. Solid DANGER instead of tinted gives the worst severity a weight the others do not have — and it is the reason the four-token status ramp exists.

What none of these do is add colour that means nothing. Every field above is either chrome that holds no data (the rail) or a direct read of state (the band, the icons, the badge). A decorative gradient would fix “bland” and break “mirror”.
